# The on-body panel is a Status Board, not a HUD

The panel is mounted on the chest and faces away from the player, so it cannot be read during a Run. It is therefore treated as a Status Board: its job is to show Score, Hearts, Link state, and battery between Runs, and to anyone watching. In-Run feedback is carried by the buzzer and by the game page's sound, not by the panel.

## Consequences

Polish spent on the panel's in-Run legibility is wasted, so it should not get much. The buzzer is a load-bearing part of the experience rather than a nicety. A wrist-mounted display later would be a separate product change, not a repositioning of this panel.
