# Sense the torso, worn on the chest

The Device is worn against the chest. A Jump is read as a vertical acceleration spike; a Crawl as a sustained forward pitch of the torso. Chest mounting makes the Intents hard to fake — arm movement cannot produce them — and a crouch is a large, clean change in torso angle.

## Considered options

- **Held in hand** — turns "jump" into "raise your arm", which is not the game being built.
- **Ankle or thigh** — good for a Jump, poor for a Crawl.
- **Waist or hip** — viable, and the closest runner-up; rejected because the chest gives a cleaner pitch signal.
